Accessibility.

Updated: 17 October 2026

Tutaluy Ltd. is committed to making this site usable by people with disabilities, in line with Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law 5758-1998 and its service-accessibility regulations (2013).

Conformance

The site is built to Israeli Standard SI 5568, based on WCAG 2.1 level AA: full keyboard navigation with a visible focus indicator, alternative text for images, semantic headings and form labels, sufficient colour contrast, text that scales to 200% without loss of content, and layouts that work on small screens. The home-page animation respects the operating system's "reduce motion" setting.

Known limitations

The bag-buffering calculator (Hebrew) updates its results live while you type; if you use a screen reader and find it hard to follow, we are happy to run the numbers for you by phone.

Found a problem?

If something on the site is not accessible to you, or you have a suggestion, tell us and we will fix it promptly: guy@tutaluy.com · +972 54-632-4746.
